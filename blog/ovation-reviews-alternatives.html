Quick summary

Ovation focuses heavily on SMS surveys and CRM features. RateTap focuses purely on the point of sale: capturing Google reviews instantly via NFC, routing negative feedback to managers privately, and giving servers true attribution.

What do Ovation reviews say?

Ovation is a robust platform that focuses on the '2-question SMS survey'. According to Ovation reviews and user feedback, operators appreciate its detailed dashboards and integrations with online ordering platforms.

However, the most common friction points involve SMS fatigue (guests not wanting text messages), lower conversion rates to actual Google reviews (because Google requires a second step after the SMS), and the high monthly subscription cost.

The problem with SMS-based review capture

Ovation relies on capturing a phone number, sending a text, waiting for a reply, and then asking the guest to click a link to go to Google.

Every step in that flow loses a percentage of happy guests. By the time the text arrives, the guest is already out of the restaurant and the emotional high of the great meal has passed.

Why RateTap is the ultimate Ovation alternative

RateTap bypasses the SMS entirely. Instead of collecting data for a later text, the server presents an NFC card at the end of the meal. The guest taps their phone, and if they had a 5-star experience, they are taken directly to Google.

This in-moment capture boasts a significantly higher conversion rate for public reviews.

  • Zero SMS fatigue or privacy concerns for guests
  • Capture happens in the restaurant while sentiment is highest
  • True server-level attribution via individual NFC cards
  • No massive software learning curve for staff

Intercepting complaints: A shared feature with a different approach

Both platforms allow you to intercept negative feedback privately. Ovation does this through their text survey. RateTap does it directly on the guest's phone during the tap.

With RateTap, if the guest selects 1 to 3 stars, the Google link is hidden. Instead, they get a private feedback form that alerts the manager instantly—allowing the manager to fix the issue before the guest even leaves the table.
