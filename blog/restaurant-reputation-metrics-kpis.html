Quick summary

Stop staring at your average star rating. Start tracking Review Capture Rate, Complaint Interception Rate, Staff Attribution Volume, and Recent 5-Star Velocity to fix service issues before they go public.

1. Review Capture Rate: Are you converting happy guests?

Most restaurants have hundreds of happy guests each week, but only capture one or two public reviews. The Review Capture Rate measures what percentage of your tables are actually leaving a review.

If this number is low, your team is likely forgetting to ask, or your request process (like an email the next day) has too much friction.

The Fix

Ask at the table while the positive moment is still fresh. Make it a one-tap process.

2. Complaint Interception Rate: Catching issues before Google

A 1-star review on Google means a guest left angry and had no other outlet. Your Complaint Interception Rate tracks how many low-intent or unhappy guests you redirect to a private feedback channel instead of a public forum.

High interception means your managers are getting the chance to make it right before the shift ends.

3. Recent 5-Star Velocity: Pushing down bad history

Your overall average moves slowly, but customers look at your most recent reviews to judge your current quality. Recent 5-Star Velocity tracks how many 5-star reviews you generate per week.

High velocity ensures that even if a rare bad review slips through, it gets buried quickly by genuine, positive feedback.

4. Staff Attribution: Who is driving your reputation?

If you can't tell which server prompted a 5-star review, you can't coach your team. Tracking review generation by staff member shows you exactly who owns the positive close-out and who might be rushing the final touchpoint.

  • Reward staff who consistently generate positive feedback
  • Identify patterns where certain shifts lag behind
  • Keep accountability fair without relying on aggressive quotas

5. Manager Resolution Time: Closing the loop

When a guest submits private feedback, the clock starts. Manager Resolution Time tracks how quickly your floor leaders address intercepted complaints. If this stretches into the next day, the guest may still take their frustration to Google.

Questions about reputation metrics

Is the overall star rating useless?

No, it is highly visible to customers. But it is a lagging indicator. You need leading indicators (like Capture Rate) to influence the final score.

How do we track Complaint Interception Rate?

You need a review platform that offers a dual-path workflow: routing happy guests to Google and unhappy guests to a private manager inbox.
