Shot chip, caption and mobile zoom

Every product shot carries a .shot__chip ("Demo data"). English pages add a .shot-caption under the frame. At 600px and below, .shot--zoom shows one region of the same image at a readable size. Pick the region with a .zoom-* class (kpi, complaint, totals, staff, live, guests, campaign, email). Resize this page to see it.
