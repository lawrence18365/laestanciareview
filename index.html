How It Works

Three simple steps to transform your team into a review-generating machine

01

Give each staff member an NFC card

They keep it in their check presenter or pocket. When it's time to drop the bill, they say: "Hey, if you had a good time, would you mind tapping this and leaving us a quick review?"

What happens: Staff present card → Guest taps phone → Magic begins
02

Guest taps, rates, and leaves feedback

They land on your branded page (no app needed), tap a star rating and can add a quick comment. You get the rating, comment, which staff member asked, and when.

Data captured: Rating • Comment • Staff ID • Timestamp • Table info
03

You get more reviews + fewer surprises

Happy guests are encouraged to post on Google. Unhappy guests share issues privately so you can respond. You see live data, leaderboards, and trends in your dashboard.

Smart routing: 5★ → Google • 1-4★ → Private manager alert

05 · Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Will our staff actually use it?

Every card is tied to a specific person, and you can see who gets reviews. Owners usually run simple contests ("Most reviews this month wins X") and adoption goes way up.

Do guests need to download an app?

No. They just tap the card with their phone and the page opens in their browser. It takes about 10 seconds total.

What about older guests or phones without NFC?

Each card also has a QR code backup. Anyone can scan it with their camera app if NFC doesn't work.

Is this compliant with Google's review rules?

We're built to encourage more reviews while respecting platform policies. You collect private feedback first, then invite guests to share their experience publicly if they'd like.

How long does setup take?

Most restaurants are up and running in under 15 minutes. We handle the page setup, you just hand out the cards and your staff can start using them immediately.

Can I use this for multiple locations?

Absolutely. The dashboard lets you filter and compare performance across all your locations from one place.
