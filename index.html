The Service Recovery Gap

Your guest is unhappy. Management finds out too late.

Guests often say nothing to the floor. By the time management sees the complaint, the guest is gone or the review is public.

Good ratings can hide bad experiences

A 4- or 5-star score can still carry an actionable complaint. Severity comes from what the guest wrote, not stars alone.

Complaints need the right owner

Group-wide noise slows action. The location GM should see the issue first, with regional and owner escalation only when nobody responds.

Response coverage is invisible

Without a per-location view of open and answered issues, operators cannot tell which teams are responding and which guests are waiting.

The Process

A guest taps at the table and writes what happened. RateTap reads the feedback for actionable service issues, including 4- and 5-star submissions.

03

Track response

If nobody acts, the issue escalates to the regional manager and owner after 2 hours for urgent complaints and 8 hours for other actionable feedback. Operators see response status by location.
