The Review Problem

Great service, but quiet guests.

Your best nights rarely become reviews. For every guest who posts on Google, dozens leave happy but silent — and you have no idea which waiter drove them away or impressed them.

Reviews Go Uncaptured

Happy guests leave the table with every intention to write a review — then never do. The ask simply isn't part of service.

No Per-Staff Attribution

When a review does land, you can't connect it to a shift, a server, or a location. You're flying blind on staff performance.

Gaps Hide Complaints

Fewer reviews means a slow rating decline you don't notice until reservations start slowing down.
