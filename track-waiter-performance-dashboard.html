Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about RateTap

How much does it cost?

+

RateTap starts at $99/month for up to 10 staff members, including NFC cards, dashboard access, and unlimited reviews. Additional staff cards are $5/month each. We also offer annual plans with 2 months free.

How long does setup take?

+

Setup is incredibly fast. Once you sign up, we ship your NFC cards within 2 business days. The dashboard setup takes about 15 minutes, and staff training takes 5 minutes per person. Most restaurants are fully operational within a week.

Do guests need to download an app?

+

No app required! Guests simply tap the NFC card with their smartphone (works with all modern iPhones and Android devices). They're taken directly to a mobile-optimized review page in their web browser.

What happens with negative reviews?

+

Reviews of 4 stars and below are routed privately to management through our dashboard. You get instant alerts so you can respond quickly and resolve issues before they become public Google reviews.

Can I track individual staff performance?

+

Absolutely! Each NFC card is unique to the staff member. You can see how many reviews each person generates, their average rating, response rates, and trends over time. It's perfect for performance reviews and incentive programs.

What if an NFC card gets lost or damaged?

+

No problem! We can deactivate lost cards instantly and ship replacements within 24 hours. Your data is safe, and there's no security risk since no personal information is stored on the cards themselves.
