Three of the strongest results

Premium steakhouse · Juárez, Mexico

Estancia Juárez: 618 guest reviews captured

Estancia Juárez has a strong service team, but most happy guests used to leave without ever posting a review, and the occasional complaint never reached the manager in time to fix it.

What changed: every server was issued a personal NFC card. Guests with a great meal tap once and land on the Google review form. Guest issues page the manager in real time so problems get fixed at the table. The location now sits at 4.7★ on Google.

Top location for review capture on the platform
Flagship location · Angelópolis, Puebla

Most reviews captured of any location

The Angelópolis flagship handles the highest cover count in the group. Pre-RateTap, only a fraction of those guests left a review. It's now the platform's top-performing location for review capture.

What changed: the host stand and every server station became a tap point. The dashboard's staff leaderboard turns review collection into friendly competition between servers. The location now sits at 4.8★ on Google.

Top-rated location · Regio Norte

Highest live rating in the group, at 4.8 stars

Regio Norte holds the highest current Google rating of any location on the platform, 4.8★. With a smaller review pool, it captures a steady stream of authentic guest reviews.

What changed: RateTap catches the bulk of their happy diners on the way out, before they have a chance to forget. A larger share of feedback is routed to the manager on shift and handled in real time for fast recovery.
