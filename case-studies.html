Three of the strongest results

Estancia Juárez: 746 guest reviews captured

Estancia Juárez has a strong service team, but most happy guests used to leave without ever posting a review, and the occasional complaint never reached the manager in time to fix it.

What changed: every server was issued a personal NFC card. After tapping, every guest can choose to leave a Google review or send private feedback to management. Private feedback pages the manager in real time so problems can be fixed at the table. The location now sits at 4.7★ on Google.
